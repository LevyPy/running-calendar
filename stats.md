## 🛠️ Maintenance Tips

1. **Updating annual summary:**
   - Add new races to the appropriate year
   - Update total distance, races completed, and PRs set

2. **Adding race category data:**
   - Update the race count for each distance category
   - Recalculate best and average times when new races are completed

3. **Tracking year-over-year progress:**
   - Add new years to the progress tables
   - Calculate improvement compared to previous best time

4. **Performance analysis:**
   - Add notes about how different conditions affect your performance
   - Use this data to better prepare for future races
