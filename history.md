## 📝 Maintenance Tips

### Adding a Completed Race

1. Create a new entry under the appropriate year in "Completed Races"
2. Use the collapsible format with detailed table
3. Include all race information, performance metrics, and links to activities
4. Add any notable achievements or personal records

### Yearly Review

1. At the end of each year, review all races
2. Create a new year section for the upcoming year
3. Archive completed races in their respective year sections
